Sheet 02 · The framework

The belonging blueprint

Belonging is a basic human need. It's a feeling, so it can't be faked or handed to you. It gets built from real pieces and renewed as you change. Here's the plan NinerU builds with you, layer by layer.

The research underneath

Where this comes from

This framework adapts “A Blueprint for College Students’ Sense of Belonging” by Dr. Terrell L. Strayhorn and J’Quen Johnson (The EDU Ledger, April 2025). It's based on their NASPA 2025 session and Strayhorn's sense of belonging theory.

They make three moves that hold up: meet basic needs, design spaces that reflect who students are, and create positive connections, where both how often and how warm those connections are count. Underneath all three: belonging has to be renewed as students move through college.

They call it a blueprint, not the blueprint. NinerU is one campus's version, grounded in UNC Charlotte's own resources, CHESS curriculum and alumni data.
